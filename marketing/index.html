How it works

One job, start to finish, without retyping anything.

Each step picks up where the last one left off, so a customer's details are entered once.

  1. Capture

    Add the customer and site, with search and history.

  2. Quote

    Line-item quotes, sent as a branded PDF.

  3. Schedule

    Book the visit and assign a technician, or make it repeat.

  4. Inspect

    The technician files the report on site: findings, photos, products, signatures.

  5. Invoice

    The invoice is raised as soon as the job is signed off.

  6. Get paid

    Record part payments, and get reminders about overdue invoices.

Features

Everything the office and the field need.

Made for small and mid-sized UK operators, not adapted from a generic field-service tool.

Inspection & treatment reports

Pest activity, findings with photo evidence, priority, and who needs to act. Hygiene and proofing, products with active ingredient and HSE/MAPP number, safety advice, and signatures.

Scheduling & repeating jobs

A calendar with technician assignment. Weekly, fortnightly, monthly or quarterly contracts book a year ahead and keep UK clock time across the clock changes.

Quotes & invoicing

Numbered quotes and invoices, part payments and overdue tracking. A single switch for VAT: nothing charges VAT until you are registered, then invoices become tax invoices.

Email from your own address

Send quotes, invoices and reports with the PDF attached, through Microsoft 365 or your own mail server. Templates are editable, and every document keeps a send history.

Reminders that run themselves

Tomorrow's bookings, imminent visits, overdue and soon-due invoices, job follow-ups and quotes about to expire, all shown in the app without being set up.

Find anything

One search across customers, quotes, bookings, jobs and invoices. Your branding, logo and colour appear on every PDF you send.

Mobile app

Your technicians' whole day, in their pocket.

The PestBase app for Android and iPhone is built for use on site: big buttons, the camera one tap away, and nothing a technician doesn't need.

  • Today's visits, with one tap to call the site contact or get directions.
  • Photos straight from the phone's camera, attached to each finding.
  • Products picked from your own list, with HSE/MAPP numbers already filled in.
  • The customer signs on screen, or the technician records why they couldn't.
  • Share the finished PDF report by email, WhatsApp or to Files.
