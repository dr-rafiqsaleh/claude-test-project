1. Introduction

SERVICE RECORD LTD operates the public PestBase website available at https://pestbase.co.uk (the "Website") and the PestBase pest control management platform available at https://app.pestbase.co.uk (the "Platform").

This Privacy Policy explains how SERVICE RECORD LTD, trading as PestBase ("PestBase", "we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses, stores, shares and otherwise processes personal data when you visit the Website, create or use an account on the Platform, or interact with any related products, services, features or communications that we make available. The Website, Platform and those related offerings are collectively referred to in this Privacy Policy as the "Services".

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal data responsibly. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our handling of your information, please email us at privacy@pestbase.co.uk.

The controller responsible for your personal data is:

Where a Customer uses the Platform to process personal data for its own purposes, the Customer is the controller and PestBase processes that personal data on the Customer's documented instructions in accordance with the data-processing terms in our Terms and Conditions. If your details were entered into PestBase by a pest control business you deal with, please contact that business first about how your data is used.

This Privacy Policy is intended to comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR"), the Data Protection Act 2018, the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations ("PECR") and any other applicable UK privacy and data-protection legislation.

5. Account Authentication and Session Management

The PestBase Platform uses passwordless sign-in: when you ask to sign in, we email you a link and a six-digit code, and either one starts a session. Sign-in and session management are provided by authentication technology operated within PestBase's own production environment. Information processed may include email addresses, account and user identifiers, authentication and session information, the time of your last sign-in, and security-related login events such as repeated requests for a sign-in code.

We use this information to provide, secure and manage access to PestBase accounts, and to limit how often sign-in codes can be requested so that the sign-in form cannot be used to send unwanted email. We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect authentication information and retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for account management, security, legal compliance and related purposes. We do not currently use an externally hosted authentication service. If this changes and an external provider processes personal data on our behalf, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly, including details of any applicable international transfers.

6. Emails Sent on Behalf of Customers

Customers can send quotes, invoices and inspection reports to their own customers from within the Platform, through the Customer's own Microsoft 365 account or mail server. When they do, we process the recipient's name and email address, the message and the attached document on the Customer's instructions, and keep a record of the email sent, including a copy of the attachment, so that the Customer has a history of what was sent and when. The credentials a Customer provides to connect its mail account are stored encrypted.

7. Support Access

PestBase support staff can access a Customer's records only during a time-limited support-access period. Normally the Customer opens that period from its settings and can close it at any time. In exceptional circumstances, such as a security incident or an urgent fault, PestBase may open emergency access for a limited period, with the reason recorded. Every access period, including emergency access, is visible to the Customer, and opening and closing access, and changes made by PestBase staff, are recorded in the Customer's audit trail.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Platform uses cookies and browser storage that are strictly necessary to provide the service you have requested: session cookies that keep you signed in, and browser storage that remembers preferences such as your chosen theme, your recent searches and, for PestBase staff, which Customer they are working in. These do not require consent under PECR.

We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies on the Website or the Platform. The Website loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests the font files from Google and Google receives your IP address as part of that request. If we introduce optional analytics or similar technologies, we will not activate them until you have given any consent required by law, and we will update this Privacy Policy.

Where your data was entered into PestBase by a Customer, we will pass your request to that Customer or help them respond to it, as they control that data.

14. Data Security

We take the security of personal information seriously and implement reasonable and appropriate technical, organisational and administrative safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised or unlawful access, disclosure, alteration, loss, destruction or misuse. These include passwordless sign-in, role-based access controls checked by our servers on every request, separation between Customers' data, encryption of stored mail-account credentials, a tamper-evident audit trail of important changes, time-limited support access that the Customer can see, and periodic review of our security practices.

15. Marketing Communications

We may send you promotional marketing communications in accordance with applicable UK GDPR and PECR requirements. Where consent is required, we will obtain it before sending the relevant promotional marketing communications.

You can opt out of marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link in the email or by emailing privacy@pestbase.co.uk.

Opting out of marketing communications will not prevent us from sending you important non-marketing communications, including sign-in emails and messages relating to your account, subscription, security, support, data export, account closure or the Services.

16. Data-Protection Complaints

If you have concerns about how PestBase processes your personal data, you can make a complaint by emailing privacy@pestbase.co.uk. We will acknowledge a data-protection complaint within 30 days, take appropriate steps to investigate it without undue delay, keep you informed where appropriate and communicate the outcome of our investigation.

You also have the right to complain directly to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), which is the UK's independent supervisory authority for data protection: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. You are not required to contact PestBase before making a complaint to the ICO.
