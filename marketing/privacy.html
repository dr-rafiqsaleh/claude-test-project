This policy explains how Service Record Ltd, trading as PestBase ("PestBase", "we"), handles personal data in the PestBase web app at app.pestbase.co.uk, the PestBase apps for Android and iOS, and this website. Service Record Ltd is registered in [England and Wales] under company number [number], at [registered address].

Who is responsible for which data

Pest control businesses ("our customers") use PestBase to manage their own customers, jobs and invoices. For that data, our customer is the controller and PestBase is their processor: we handle it only on their instructions. For the accounts of people who sign in to PestBase, and for enquiries made through this website, PestBase is the controller.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, work email address, role, and when you last signed in.
  • Business records our customers enter: their customers' names, contact details and site addresses, quotes, bookings, inspection reports, photos, signatures and invoices.
  • Photos: the mobile apps use the camera and photo library only when you choose to add a photo to a report.
  • Technical data: IP address and request logs, kept for security and troubleshooting.

The apps do not use advertising identifiers, do not track you across other apps or websites, and contain no third-party analytics.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: sign-in, storing records, generating PDFs and sending emails our customers ask us to send.
  • To keep it secure: rate-limiting sign-in, keeping an audit trail of important changes.
  • To reply to enquiries you send us.

Our lawful bases are performance of a contract and our legitimate interest in running a secure service.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to host and run PestBase: [hosting provider and region], [email delivery provider]. They process data only on our instructions. We do not sell personal data. PestBase support staff can see a customer's records only while that customer has granted support access.

How long we keep it

Records are kept while our customer's account is active and deleted [within N days] of the account closing, unless the law requires us to keep them longer (for example, invoices for tax purposes). In-app notifications are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, or object to how it is used. If your data was entered by a pest control business, contact them first: they control it. Otherwise email [privacy@pestbase.co.uk]. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Deleting your account

Your organisation's PestBase administrator can deactivate your account at any time. To ask for your account to be deleted, email [privacy@pestbase.co.uk].
