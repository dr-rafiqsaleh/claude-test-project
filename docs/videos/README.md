# PestBase videos

Three videos, recorded from the real portal with a made-up company ("Greenshield Pest Control"). None of the customers, addresses or figures are real. The videos are silent, and the words are on screen as captions, so they work with the sound off. Each one has a script below, timed to the video, for a voice-over if you want one.

## Tutorial: "Getting started with PestBase" (16:9, 4:13)

For office staff and technicians. The walkthrough runs in order: signing in, Today, customers, quotes, jobs, the technician's inspection report on a phone, then reports, invoices and payments back in the office. The same steps, with screenshots, are in the portal under **Help > Guide**.

The times below are when each caption appears. The same lines are in `pestbase-tutorial-16x9.srt`.

### Getting started with PestBase  (0:00)

*Title card: From signing in to getting paid, in a few minutes*

- **0:04–0:08** Go to your PestBase address and type the email address your administrator added for you.
- **0:08–0:10** Choose "Email me a sign-in link". There is no password to remember.
- **0:10–0:14** Open the link in the email, or type the six-digit code it contains.
- **0:14–0:20** Today is your starting point: what needs attention, today's jobs and the money coming in.
- **0:20–0:28** Choose any card to jump straight to that list.

### Customers  (0:28)

*Title card: Add the people and sites you work for*

- **0:32–0:38** Customers lists everyone you work for. Search by name, postcode or phone.
- **0:38–0:49** Add a new customer: name, contact details and the site address.
- **0:49–0:52** Then choose "Create customer".
- **0:52–0:56** The customer's page keeps their quotes, jobs, reports and invoices together.

### Quotes  (0:56)

*Title card: Price the work and send it for approval*

- **0:59–1:02** Start a quote straight from the customer's page.
- **1:02–1:13** Add a line for each piece of work: pest, service, quantity and price.
- **1:13–1:18** Totals, including VAT, update as you type.
- **1:18–1:20** Choose "Send quote" to email it to the customer as a PDF.
- **1:20–1:26** Check the message, then send it.
- **1:26–1:29** When the customer says yes, mark the quote as accepted.

### Jobs  (1:29)

*Title card: Book the work and give it to a technician*

- **1:32–1:34** An accepted quote can be booked as a job in one step.
- **1:34–1:41** Add who to meet on site, when, and which technician is going.
- **1:41–1:46** Regular contracts can repeat weekly, monthly or quarterly.
- **1:46–1:51** The technician is told about the job straight away, in PestBase and by email.
- **1:51–1:55** Once the customer has agreed the time, choose "Confirm".
- **1:55–1:59** Jobs lists every visit. Switch to the calendar to see the week at a glance.

### Inspection reports  (2:01)

*Title card: Technicians fill them in on site, on their phone*

- **2:05–2:10** On site, open PestBase in your phone's browser. Today lists your visits in order.
- **2:10–2:12** Choose "Start job" when you arrive.
- **2:12–2:16** The inspection report opens, ready to fill in from top to bottom.
- **2:16–2:21** Record the visit type and the level of pest activity.
- **2:21–2:35** Describe what you found, then add a finding for each area.
- **2:35–2:40** Add photos straight from the phone's camera.
- **2:40–2:50** Note what you did and every product used, for your records and the customer's.
- **2:50–2:57** Finally, you and the customer sign on the screen.
- **2:57–3:02** Choose "Complete job". The report goes to the office and an invoice is raised.

### Reports  (3:05)

*Title card: Check the technician's report and send it to the customer*

- **3:08–3:13** Back in the office, Today shows the report is in and ready to check.
- **3:13–3:22** The completed report has everything the technician recorded, with photos and signatures.
- **3:22–3:28** Choose "Email report" to send it to the customer as a PDF.

### Invoices  (3:28)

*Title card: Get paid for the work*

- **3:31–3:35** Completing the job raised a draft invoice from the quoted price.
- **3:35–3:41** Check it, then choose "Send invoice". It goes by email with the PDF attached.
- **3:41–3:50** When money arrives, choose "Record payment". Part payments are fine.
- **3:50–3:56** The invoice shows what has been paid and what is still owed.
- **3:56–4:02** Invoices lists everything you have billed. PestBase reminds you when payments are due or overdue.
